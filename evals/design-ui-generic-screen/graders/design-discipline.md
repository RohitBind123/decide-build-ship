PASS if the reply does all of these: proposes writing design rules or a design system (tokens for type, colour, spacing, with light and dark themes) and asks the owner for direction such as brand adjectives or admired products; covers screen states including empty, loading and error; includes accessibility (contrast, keyboard or focus); and plans to review the real screens, for example from screenshots.
FAIL if the reply only changes colours or fonts on one screen, or ignores empty, loading and error states.
