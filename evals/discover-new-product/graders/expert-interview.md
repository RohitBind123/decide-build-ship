PASS if the reply does all of these: frames the product (who it is for, the one job, stage such as MVP); researches or proposes to research the domain and competing products, or clearly marks domain facts as unverified; asks the owner questions where each has a recommended answer; suggests at least one feature the owner did not mention (for example scheduling, reminders, invoicing, client records, an admin screen); and names the documents it will write (such as a product brief or PRD, architecture, and a roadmap of milestones).
FAIL if the reply writes implementation code, picks a final tech stack without asking, or asks bare questions with no recommendations.
