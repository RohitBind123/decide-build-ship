Discovery runs over several turns: framing, research, interview rounds, then documents. Judge the reply as the start of that process.

PASS if the reply does all of these: frames the product (restates the idea, or asks about who it is for, the stage such as MVP, or the platform); researches the domain and competing products, says it will do so, or clearly marks domain facts as unverified; asks the owner questions where each has a recommended answer; and names the documents it will write (such as a product brief or PRD, architecture, and a roadmap of milestones).

FAIL if the reply writes implementation code, picks a final tech stack without asking, asks bare questions with no recommendations, or invents legal, tax or pricing facts as if checked.
